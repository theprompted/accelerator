1. Metal Conducts Every Degree — Straight Into Your Lips

The same property that makes it "durable" makes it painful to drink through.

Here's something nobody mentions in the product listing.

Metal is an excellent conductor of heat.

Those loosely bound electrons that make stainless steel feel "solid" also transfer temperature directly to your mouth.

Hot coffee becomes a metal straw that burns your lips.

Iced drinks become a frozen rod against your teeth.

Think about what you do with a hot pan.

You grab the handle — and immediately let go.

The metal conducted the heat straight through.

You don't keep grabbing the handle thinking "maybe this time it won't burn."

You get a different tool.

Your metal straw is the hot pan handle you keep grabbing — except it's against your lips.

You shouldn't have to wait for your drink to reach a "safe straw temperature" before taking a sip.

The straw should work with any drink, at any temperature, without becoming a liability.
