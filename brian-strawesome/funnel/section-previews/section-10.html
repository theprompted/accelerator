Meet Strawesome — The Original Glass Straw Company

Most glass straws on the market don't qualify.

They're mass-produced overseas. They skip proper annealing. They're thin, cloudy, and backed by no real warranty.

But one company has been doing this differently since before anyone cared about reusable straws.

In 2009, a woman named Daedra in Michigan started making glass straws by hand.

Back then, almost nobody was talking about reusable straws.

Strawesome wasn't riding a trend. They were creating one.

Sixteen years later, they're the longest-running dedicated glass straw maker in the world.

Every Strawesome straw is:

  • Handcrafted by Daedra in the family workshop
  • Properly annealed in a kiln for strength
  • Seamless construction (no welded joints)
  • Transparent (you can see it's clean)
  • Non-reactive (nothing leaches)
  • Backed by a lifetime warranty
