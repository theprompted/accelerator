4. That Metallic Taste Isn't Your Imagination

Research shows chromium and nickel dissolve into acidic drinks with every sip.

That metallic taste you notice with your morning coffee isn't nothing.

It's metal dissolving into your drink.

Research shows that chromium and nickel — both present in stainless steel — leach into beverages with high acidity.

Your orange juice. Your smoothie. Your iced coffee.

The higher the temperature, the more it leaches.

Think about "filtered" water that still tastes like the pipes.

The filter removed what it was supposed to remove.

But the water still picks up taste from the faucet on the way out.

Your metal straw is the faucet ruining the filtered water.

"Stainless" steel is a marketing term — not a promise that nothing transfers.

You shouldn't have to worry about what's dissolving into your drink.

There's a material that's completely non-reactive.

Nothing leaches. Nothing transfers. Nothing taints your drink.
