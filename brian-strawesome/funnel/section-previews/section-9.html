Why Glass Works (When Done Right)

Most people are afraid of glass straws for one reason: breaking.

That fear is reasonable — for cheap glass.

Cheap glass is mass-produced without proper finishing.

And when cheap glass fails, it shatters.

But properly made glass is different.

There's a process called annealing — slowly cooling glass in a kiln to remove internal stress.

Annealed glass is stable.

Annealed glass is predictable.

Annealed glass doesn't shatter unexpectedly.

This is what separates a glass straw you should trust from one you shouldn't.

When glass is annealed correctly — in a proper kiln, by someone who understands the material — you get something remarkable.

The glass straw you were afraid of is actually the safer choice.

You just need to find one that's made right.
