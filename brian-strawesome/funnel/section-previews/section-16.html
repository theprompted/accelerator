Reusable Should Mean Forever

The sustainability promise got watered down somewhere along the way.

"Reusable" started meaning "replace less often."

People buy reusable straws, then buy more reusable straws when those break.

That's not reusable. That's just slower disposable.

Strawesome believes reusable should mean you never buy another one.

That's why they only make glass — it lasts.

That's why they anneal properly — so it doesn't break.

That's why they offer a lifetime warranty — they stand behind "forever."

That's why they've been doing this for 16 years — it takes time to perfect "forever."

That's what the 1.5% replacement rate proves — "forever" is real.
