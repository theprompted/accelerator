Frequently Asked Questions

How do I clean my glass straw?
Every Strawesome set comes with a cleaning brush designed for your straw. Quick rinse after use keeps things fresh. For deeper cleaning, run it through the dishwasher — our straws are fully dishwasher safe. The best part: you can SEE when it's clean.
Will it really not break?
Our glass is properly annealed — slowly cooled in a kiln to remove internal stress. This is what cheap glass skips. The result: a 1.5% replacement rate over 16 years. And if yours ever does break, our lifetime warranty covers it.
Can I use it with hot drinks?
Yes. Glass doesn't conduct heat like metal. Your lips touch the glass, not the temperature of your drink. Hot coffee, iced tea, room temperature water — all comfortable.
What if it doesn't fit my tumbler?
We make straws specifically sized for Stanley, YETI, Hydro Flask, and Owala tumblers. Check our Tumbler Replacement collection for your exact fit. If you're not sure, reach out — we're happy to help you find the right size.
How is this different from cheap glass straws on Amazon?
Three things: (1) Annealing — we properly anneal every straw in a kiln. Cheap glass skips this step. (2) Handmade — Daedra makes every straw by hand in Michigan. (3) Lifetime Warranty — we back every straw forever. Amazon glass comes with no warranty.
Do you ship internationally?
Yes. We ship worldwide. Free USA shipping on orders $49+. International shipping rates calculated at checkout.
