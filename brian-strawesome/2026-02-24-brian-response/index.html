Your Questions

Are we setting up landing pages that aren't linked on the website?
Nah. It's usually hosted on a subdomain using a landing page builder. But so you can get up and running faster, you can use Shopify Pages to build it out, and it still lives on your website.

Once that proves viable, we put up additional for different audiences?
Yup. My recommendation is to pop up 2-4 funnels per month, and to test 200 ads per funnel.

Does any of this eventually get to the website and product pages?
Yeah — in your case you can build directly in Shopify Pages. And the CTA buttons on the offer page can go to your PDPs or Collection Pages. And from another angle, what you learn from testing these funnels can be transferred over to the PDPs and non-funnel pages — copy that's working, headlines that are working, images that are working. So it "makes it over" in more than one way.
