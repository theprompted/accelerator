Answering Your Questions

Q: Are we setting up landing pages not linked on the website?

Yes. The presell page is a standalone landing page. It's not in your site navigation. Ads drive directly to it. This is intentional — you're testing a funnel separate from your main site so you can measure what works without affecting your existing traffic.

Q: Once it proves viable, do we add more for different audiences?

Yes. The pattern is: one thesis, one funnel, test it. If it works, scale it. Then build a second funnel with a different angle for a different audience segment. But don't build the second one until the first one proves out.

Q: Does this eventually get to the website and product pages?

Eventually, yes. Once you find what converts, you can pull winning elements into your main site. But right now the goal is to find a winning funnel — not redesign your website. Keep them separate for now.
