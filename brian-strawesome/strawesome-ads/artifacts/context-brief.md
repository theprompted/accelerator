## KEY CLAIMS / EVIDENCE SECTIONS

### R1: Metal Conducts Temperature — Straight Into Your Lips
- Metal is an excellent conductor of heat (loosely bound electrons)
- Hot coffee burns your lips through the straw
- Iced drinks create a "frozen rod against your teeth"
- Metaphor: "Your metal straw is the hot pan handle you keep grabbing — except it's against your lips"
- Glass doesn't conduct heat — "lets the temperature stay where it belongs"

### R2: You Can't See What's Growing Inside Metal
- Can't see through metal = can't verify cleanliness
- Research: reusable containers harbor 100,000x more bacteria than a bathroom doorknob
- E. coli, Salmonella, Listeria, Mold — all invisible in opaque tube
- Biofilm forms on interior walls
- Metaphor: "Your metal straw is a restaurant kitchen you can never inspect"
- Glass is transparent — "see it's clean with your own eyes"

### R3: Metal Doesn't Give — Your Teeth Do
- Metal doesn't flex on impact
- "One accidental bite. One clink too hard. One moment of distraction."
- Starbucks recalled 2.5 million stainless steel straws (2016) — child mouth lacerations
- Dentists warn about tooth enamel damage
- Metaphor: "Metal straws are concrete walls for your teeth"
- "Unbreakable sounds like a feature until you realize it just means something else has to break instead"

### R4: Metallic Taste — Metal Dissolves Into Drinks
- Chromium and nickel leach into acidic beverages
- Higher temperature = more leaching
- Longer contact = more leaching
- More worn straw = more leaching
- Nickel sensitivity can cause dermatitis
- "Stainless steel is a marketing term — not a promise that nothing transfers"
- Glass is non-reactive — nothing leaches

### R5: When Metal Straws Fail, They Fail Dangerously
- Elena Struthers-Gardner fatality (November 2018, England)
- Fell with Mason jar, metal straw impaled through eye socket, pierced brain, died 2 days later
- Coroner warning: metal straws should not be used with lids that hold them in place
- "On impact, a metal straw becomes a weapon"
- Metaphor: "Metal straws have no crumple zones"
- "The safest tools aren't the ones that never fail — they're the ones that fail safely"
