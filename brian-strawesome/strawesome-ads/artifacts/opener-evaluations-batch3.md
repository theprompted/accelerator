## MANDATORY SELF-CHECKS

### Expression #1 — Angle 1: Flat Denial

```
OPENER: "Metal straws aren't safer. They just feel safer. There's a difference."
TARGET: The reader's belief that choosing metal was a safety decision
READER REACTION: "Wait — I chose metal BECAUSE it's safer. Are you saying I was wrong?"
GOLD STANDARD: PASS — Attacks the identity decision (I chose safe), not the product category
FUCK YOU TEST: PASS — They want to argue "no, metal IS safer because it can't break"
TURN TEST: PASS — "Feeling safe and being safe are different things. A coroner issued a warning about metal straws after a fatality. The feeling was wrong."
FAIL PATTERN: N/A
VERDICT: PASS
```
