# Strawesome Funnel — Master Expression Bank

**Funnel:** "5 Reasons You Shouldn't Use Metal Straws"
**One Belief Attacked:** "Metal straws are safer than glass straws."
**Identity Defended:** Teachers, nurses, and moms (35-44) who switched to metal straws — they made a reasonable choice with incomplete information.
**Identity Attacked:** The post-plastic-ban consensus that metal = safe, unbreakable = good.
**Resolution Withheld:** Glass straws (Strawesome) — never named in ads.

## BATCH PLANNING RULES

1. Default batch size is **20 ads**. Each batch pulls from all 10 angles — 2 per angle.

2. Each expression has a pre-assigned evidence pairing. Within a batch of 20, aim for at least 4 different evidence pairings.

3. **Evidence key:**
   - R1: Metal conducts temperature (hot pan handle metaphor, burns/freezes lips)
   - R2: Can't see inside metal (100,000x bacteria, biofilm, opaque = hidden kitchen)
   - R3: Metal doesn't give (2.5M Starbucks recall, tooth damage, concrete wall metaphor)
   - R4: Metal leaches (chromium, nickel, acidic drinks, metallic taste)
   - R5: Metal fails dangerously (Elena Struthers-Gardner fatality, coroner warning, no crumple zones)

4. Images stay in the **eco-conscious consumer's world**: kitchen counter, tumbler with straw, kitchen drawer, morning coffee, office desk, cleaning brush, Mason jar, Stanley cup

5. Each batch must use unique image concepts — check batch-history.md to avoid repeats.

6. After each batch, append used expression numbers, evidence pairings, AND image concepts to batch-history.md.

7. No expression number is ever reused across batches.
