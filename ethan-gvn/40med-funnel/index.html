Your path from here to live ads

Same five steps as the $56k funnel, and shorter this time because the store setup carries over.

1. Download the package.
Download everything here - all 3 funnels' pages, the images, the ad copy as paste-ready text files, and step-by-step GoHighLevel instructions (START-HERE.html).
Done when: you've unzipped it and can see the ghl-package folder.

2. Shopify products - already done.
The offer pages use the same 3 tiers as the $56k funnel: 1 bottle at $69, "Kids 350mg - 3 Pack" at $149, "Kids 350mg - 6 Pack" at $269. If you built the two pack products for the $56k funnel, reuse those URLs. If not, the $56k hub has the two-duplicate walkthrough.
Done when: you have the two pack URLs handy.

3. Rebuild the six pages in GoHighLevel.
Open START-HERE.html from the package and follow Part 1 - paste each page's HTML, upload the images, swap the image links.
Done when: all six pages are live and you've written down your three page-1 URLs.

4. Point the tier buttons at your checkouts.
On each of the three offer pages, update the three buttons - 1 bottle to your Kids 350mg page, 3-pack and 6-pack to the two pack products.
Done when: you can click all three buttons on each offer page and land on the right product.

5. Build the 12 ads.
Follow Part 2 of START-HERE. 4 flexible ads per funnel, one ad set. The images are pre-sorted into folders, the copy is paste-ready text files, and each ad's destination is one of your three page-1 URLs from step 3.
Done when: all 12 ads are built and approved in Ads Manager.

Nothing here requires a decision from you. Every price, image, and line of copy is already set. This is assembly.

What's different about each funnel

Most of the mechanical parts (pivot to GVN mention + the "sales part" of the funnel) are the same. What changes is the lead-in that gets them primed for the shift.

The Ladder leads with the doses only ever going up - forty prescriptions, not one dose ever came down - as the thing that whets their appetite to shift perspective.

The Fog leads with what the medications cost the kid - quieter at school, personality fading, "quieter child, less child" - as the thing that whets their appetite to shift perspective.

The Prescription leads with the ten-minute visits and the rotating labels - anxiety becomes Tourette's becomes behavioral - as the thing that whets their appetite to shift perspective.
