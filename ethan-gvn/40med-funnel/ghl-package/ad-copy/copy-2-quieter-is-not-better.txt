A quieter kid is not the same as a better kid.

It took my family years to see the difference.

On my medications I was calmer at school and calmer at the dinner table, and my motor tics were still there.

In tenth grade a teacher found me asleep at my desk and thought I was on drugs.

That night I told my dad the truth: I can feel my personality fading.

Forty medications made me easier to manage.

None of them stopped the tics.
