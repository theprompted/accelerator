In eleven years on forty medications for my motor tics, not one dose ever came down.

Fluoxetine started at ten milligrams.

Twenty by March.

Thirty by May.

Sertraline started at twenty-five.

Then fifty.

Abilify went five, ten, twenty.

Then my mom wrote what no doctor had written: there are no improvements with increased dosage.

She stopped the Abilify herself.

That was the first time anyone stopped anything.

The logic never changed.

It is not working yet, so he needs more.

Every one of those prescriptions was aimed at the tics everyone could see.

None of them were aimed at what was causing them.
