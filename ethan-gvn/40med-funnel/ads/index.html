40 Medications: The 40 Ads

40 image ads for Facebook, shown as they would appear in the feed. Every ad leads to: "I Took 40 Medications for My Motor Tics. Here's What Finally Stopped Them."

How to launch these

Same playbook as the $56k funnel. The funnel links on this site are previews for reading, not ad destinations - your ads point at the GoHighLevel pages you set up (or at the live preview pages, which already carry your Meta pixel).

First, download the package. It has the 40 ad images already sorted into 4 folders, the 5 ad texts as ready-to-paste files, the landing pages, and step-by-step instructions.

⬇ Download the full package

Part 1 - put the pages on GoHighLevel. Open START-HERE.html from the download and follow it: three funnels, two pages each, paste the HTML, swap the image links, test on your phone. Your Meta pixel is already inside every page and carries over when you paste. When you finish you will have three page-1 URLs - one per funnel. Those are your ad destinations. If you already built the 3-Pack and 6-Pack products for the $56k funnel, the offer pages reuse those same two checkout URLs.

Part 2 - build the ads. 12 ads in one new ad set. You only build 4 from scratch; the other 8 are duplicates where you change a single link.

  1. In Ads Manager, create a new ad set. Name it 40 Medications Funnel.
  2. Create a new ad inside it. Name it Ladder - 1.
  3. Images: add every image from the download folder ad-images/flexible-ad-1 (there are 10). Adding multiple images makes it a flexible ad, which means Meta rotates all of them and finds the winners for you.
  4. Primary text: under the text box, click Add text option until you have 5 boxes. Paste one copy into each. The 5 copies are in ad-copy/ in the download.
  5. Headline: I Took 40 Medications for My Motor Tics. Here's What Finally Stopped Them.
  6. Website URL: your page-1 URL for The Ladder.
  7. Build 3 more ads exactly like it: Ladder - 2 (images from flexible-ad-2), Ladder - 3 (flexible-ad-3), Ladder - 4 (flexible-ad-4). Same 5 texts, same headline, same URL every time.

Then duplicate those 4 ads. Rename the copies Fog - 1, 2, 3, 4 and change one thing only: the website URL becomes your page-1 URL for The Fog.

Duplicate once more. Rename them Prescription - 1, 2, 3, 4 and the website URL becomes your page-1 URL for The Prescription.

That's it. 12 ads, and every funnel gets all 40 images and all 5 copies tested against it. The ad name always tells you which funnel it feeds.
