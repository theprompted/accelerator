Frequently Asked Questions

Is this like Ozempic?
No. Ozempic is a prescription GLP-1 drug — it mimics a fullness hormone and slows digestion. CBD works through a completely different system. It doesn't mimic hormones, doesn't require a prescription, and doesn't target the digestive tract. The mechanism is different at every level.
Will I fail a drug test?
No. This is CBD isolate at 0.0% THC. THC is the compound that triggers a positive drug test. It is not present in this product.
How is this different from the supplements I've tried?
The supplements you've tried all targeted your appetite directly — blocking it, suppressing it, slowing it down. This doesn't fight the appetite. It addresses the stress response and broken signaling that's been generating the appetite in the first place. Different mechanism, different level.
Is CBD legal?
Yes. CBD derived from hemp (less than 0.3% THC) is federally legal in the United States under the 2018 Farm Bill.
How long before I notice something?
Most people notice calmer evenings and better sleep within the first one to two weeks. Appetite changes tend to follow in weeks two to four. The full reset can take four to eight weeks. It depends on how long the stress response has been in overdrive. Consistency matters more than speed.
Can I take this with other medications?
Talk to your doctor before adding CBD to any existing medication regimen. CBD can interact with certain medications, particularly those processed by the liver's CYP450 enzyme system. Your doctor can advise based on your specific situation.
Do I need a subscription?
No. Every order is a one-time purchase. No auto-ship, no cancellation required.
Will CBD make me feel high or foggy?
No. This is CBD isolate with zero THC — the compound responsible for psychoactive effects. CBD works on the endocannabinoid system without any intoxicating effects.
Is this safe if I'm pregnant or breastfeeding?
No — skip it. CBD is not recommended during pregnancy or breastfeeding. If you're pregnant, nursing, or trying to conceive, talk to your doctor before using any CBD product.
