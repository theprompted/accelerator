Part 1: Funnel Architecture

The Flow

Education Page (5 Reasons) → Offer Page → Checkout

Education Page: Destroys the old belief ("exercise fixes anxiety"), establishes the new frame ("it's a nervous system problem, not an energy problem"), creates curiosity about the solution.

Offer Page: Reveals the solution (CBD/Endocannabinoid System), addresses objections to CBD, introduces the product, stacks proof, presents bundles.

Why This Order

You can't sell a CBD product to someone who still believes exercise is the answer. The Education Page does belief demolition before product introduction. By the time they see the product, they already need what it does.

The Unifying Thread: The Alarm Metaphor

The entire funnel uses one metaphor: your dog's nervous system is an alarm stuck on. Exercise can't turn it off. The alarm isn't broken - it's starving for what it needs to reset. CBD feeds the reset mechanism.

This metaphor carries from the Education Page through the Offer Page. Every section reinforces it. When you build your next funnel, find one metaphor that explains the whole problem-solution arc.

The 3 Core Belief Shifts

"A tired dog is a calm dog" → "Exhaustion and calm are different systems"
"My dog has a behavior problem" → "My dog has a biochemistry problem"
"I haven't tried hard enough" → "I was using the wrong tool"

Section 2: Reason 1 - The Science Origin Story

"A Tired Dog Is a Calm Dog" Was Never Based on Science

Think about what we knew about dog anxiety in 1970.

Almost nothing.

We didn't know dogs had stress hormones. We didn't know their brains could get stuck in panic mode...

"A tired dog is a calm dog" comes from the same era as shock collars and dominance theory.

The advice wasn't wrong for its time. It was made for a different problem - excess energy, not chemical panic.

What this does: Explains why the old advice exists. Instead of just saying "it's wrong," you show when it was created and what it was designed for. This makes the reader feel like they're getting insider knowledge.

Key Technique

The Origin Story Reframe. When you explain where bad advice came from, you turn enemies into misguided friends. The reader doesn't feel stupid for following it - they feel informed for learning better.

Lines Worth Studying

  • "The first studies on canine cortisol wouldn't come for another two decades." — Specific detail creates credibility.
  • "One approach exhausts the body and hopes the mind follows. The other asks: is the nervous system capable of calming down at all?" — The Wise Distinction pattern.

Section 4: Reason 3 - Wild Dogs Comparison

Wild Dogs Don't Run Marathons to Stay Calm

Argument from Nature. People trust what's "natural." Wild dogs are the control group that proves the point.

Lines Worth Studying
