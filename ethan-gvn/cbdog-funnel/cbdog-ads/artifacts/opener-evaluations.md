## FULL SELF-CHECK EVALUATIONS

### Expression #1: "Exercise doesn't calm anxious dogs."
**Angle:** 1-Flat Denial
**Evidence Pairing:** R2 (Cortisol stays elevated after exercise)

**OPENER:** "Exercise doesn't calm anxious dogs."
**TARGET:** The belief that exercise = calm. The reader has structured their entire routine around this assumption.
**READER REACTION:** "Wait, what? I've been walking my dog 5 miles a day specifically BECAUSE I believe exercise calms them."
**GOLD STANDARD:** PASS — Directly denies the core belief they've built their routine around.
**FUCK YOU TEST:** PASS — The reader's immediate reaction is defensive: "Yes it does, that's why I do it."
**TURN TEST:** PASS — Turn: "It exhausts them. That's not the same thing. Their nervous system is still firing."
**FAIL PATTERN:** N/A
**VERDICT:** PASS
