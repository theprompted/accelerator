### AD 1: "Exercise doesn't calm anxious dogs." (Flat Denial, R2)

**CONCEPT: Still Panting on the Dog Bed**

**DESCRIPTION:** Dog collapsed on dog bed, tongue out, still panting heavily, with tennis ball nearby on messy floor.

**SUBJECT TYPE:** Object (the scene carries contradiction, dog is incidental to the point)

**CONTRADICTION:** Evidence of Failure — The dog is exhausted from exercise but shows signs of restlessness (still panting hard, alert eyes). The contradiction: tired but not calm.

**LAYER 1 (TECHNICAL):** Yes. Shot from standing looking down at the dog bed. Harsh overhead light creating shadows. Dog bed pushed against wall, some dog hair visible, floor has scuff marks.

**LAYER 2 (CONTEXT):** Yes. Caption: "We just walked 3 miles. This is 10 minutes later." Someone would take this to show friends that their dog is still wired after a long walk.

**LAYER 3 (INTENT):** Yes. Immediate recognition — "that's my dog." The panting tongue + collapsed position = visual contradiction of tired but not calm.

**OPENER MATCH:** "Exercise doesn't calm anxious dogs" — the image shows the exact failure. Dog is physically exhausted but still showing stress signals. Matches the EMOTIONAL STATE (the futility of exhaustion).

**FACE TEST:** No human face. Dog's state is incidental — the contradiction (exhausted + still panting/alert) carries the weight.

**STOCK CHECK:** No. Stock libraries have "dog on bed" or "tired dog" — not the specific contradiction of freshly-exercised-but-still-stressed with messy floor context.

**VERDICT:** PASS
