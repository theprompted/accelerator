## BATCH PLANNING RULES

1. Default batch size is **20 ads**. Each batch pulls from all 10 angles — 2 per angle. Scale proportionally for other sizes. Every angle must be represented at least once.

2. Each expression has a pre-assigned evidence pairing (R1-R5). Within a batch of 20, aim for at least 4 different evidence pairings — no more than 6 ads using the same Reason.

3. **Evidence key:**
   - R1: "Tired dog = calm dog" was never based on science (1970s, pre-dates cortisol research)
   - R2: Studies show cortisol stays elevated after exercise (physical fatigue ≠ nervous system regulation)
   - R3: Wild dogs don't run marathons — they have something in their biology that resets stress
   - R4: You can walk miles every day and still have an anxious dog (mopping vs. fixing the leak)
   - R5: The dog isn't anxious — they're running low on something (stuck alarm metaphor)

4. Images stay in the **anxious dog owner's world**: leashes by the door, exhausted dog after walk, tennis balls, dog park scenes, worn-out sneakers, panting dog, empty water bowl after exercise, anxious dog behaviors (pacing, panting, whale eye).

5. Each batch must use unique image concepts — check batch-history.md to avoid repeating visual setups.

6. After each batch, append used expression numbers, evidence pairings, AND image concepts to batch-history.md.

7. No expression number is ever reused across batches.
