OPTION B ENHANCED: Badges + Rich Descriptions

Each section has 3 lines: what it does, why it matters, and the key technique. Substantial enough to be useful as a reference.

Education Page (Sections 1-7)

1
Hero + Opening Violation

Challenges their core belief: "A tired dog is a calm dog" is wrong - you're making it worse.
Creates cognitive tension that demands resolution.
The reader must either dismiss you or keep reading.

Opening Violation
2
Reason 1 - The Science Origin Story

Explains where the bad advice came from.
It was made in 1970 for excess energy, not chemical panic.
The reader feels informed rather than stupid - they weren't wrong, the advice was outdated.

Origin Story Reframe
3
Reason 2 - Research Evidence

Provides scientific proof.
Cortisol studies show exhausted dogs still have anxiety - the alarm is still blaring.
Data convinces the logical readers, then a relatable metaphor makes it stick.

Research + Metaphor
4
Reason 3 - Wild Dogs Comparison

Uses nature as evidence.
Wolves rest 16 hours a day and aren't anxious - same species, different results.
If calm came from exhaustion, wild dogs would be anxiety-ridden. They're not.

Argument from Nature
5
Reason 4 - The Mop vs Leak Analogy

Validates their effort and removes guilt.
You're not lazy - you've been using the wrong tool. Exercise is the mop, not the fix.
No amount of mopping fixes a leak. This is the emotional turning point.

Wrong Tool Analogy
6
Reason 5 - The Biochemical Gap

Completes the alarm metaphor.
The reset mechanism isn't broken - it's starved of what it needs.
"Depleted" implies something that can be refilled. This reframe makes the solution feel obvious.

Problem Completion
7
Cliffhanger + CTA

Creates the open loop.
You know the problem - click to learn the solution.
The brain hates incomplete patterns. You've built the question; the offer page answers it.

Open Loop

Offer Page (Sections 8-13)

8
Solution Reveal

Names what they've never heard of: the Endocannabinoid System.
This is what resets the alarm.
Naming creates power - "ECS" sounds scientific and specific, more credible than vague promises.

Mechanism Reveal
9
Bridge + CBD Introduction

Introduces CBD and immediately handles objections.
It's not marijuana. It's not a sedative. It won't get them high.
Address the biggest concern in the same breath as the reveal - before skepticism crystallizes.

Objection at Reveal
10
Most Calming Treats Fail

Explains why past "calming treats" didn't work.
Wrong ingredients (hemp seed oil ≠ CBD), wrong dose (not therapeutic), no verification.
Creates a checklist the reader uses to evaluate your product.

Pre-Answer Objections
11
Meet CBDog + Ingredients

Introduces the product.
Every feature directly answers a problem seeded in Section 10.
Full-spectrum CBD? Check. Therapeutic dose? Check. The product feels inevitable, not pitched.

Feature-Benefit Match
12
Three Benefits + Timeline

Future-paces the result.
Week 1: subtle shifts. Week 2-3: the doorbell rings and the meltdown doesn't come.
Week 4-6: you realize you've stopped bracing for panic. Specificity creates believability.

Timeline Future Pacing
13
Bundles + Guarantee + Final CTA

Removes risk with 365-day guarantee.
Your dog gets calmer, or you get your money back.
A guarantee this long signals confidence. Bundle options create a value decision, not a yes/no decision.

Risk Reversal
