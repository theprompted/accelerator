The $56k Hook: The 34 Ads

34 image ads for Facebook, shown as they would appear in the feed. Every ad leads to: "My Mom Spent $56,576 Trying to Stop My Motor Tics. Here's What Finally Worked for Me."

How to launch these

Two parts: put the pages on GoHighLevel, then build the ads. The funnel links on this site are previews for reading, not ad destinations - your ads will point at the GoHighLevel pages you set up.

First, download the package. It has the 34 ad images already sorted into 4 folders, the ad text as ready-to-paste files, the landing pages, and a guide that opens in your browser (START-HERE.html) with the page setup steps.

⬇ Download the full package (11 MB)

Part 1 - put the pages on GoHighLevel. Open START-HERE.html from the download and follow it: three funnels, two pages each, paste the HTML, swap the image links, test on your phone. Your Meta pixel is already inside every page and carries over when you paste. When you finish you will have three page-1 URLs - one per funnel. Those are your ad destinations.

Part 2 - build the ads. 12 ads in one new ad set. You only build 4 from scratch; the other 8 are duplicates where you change a single link.

  1. In Ads Manager, create a new ad set. Name it $56k Funnel.
  2. Create a new ad inside it. Name it Argument - 1.
  3. Images: add every image from the download folder ad-images/flexible-ad-1 (there are 9). Adding multiple images makes it a flexible ad, which means Meta rotates all of them and finds the winners for you.
  4. Primary text: under the text box, click Add text option until you have 5 boxes. Paste one copy into each. The 5 copies are in ad-copy/ in the download.
  5. Headline: My Mom Spent $56,576 Trying to Stop My Motor Tics. Here's What Finally Worked for Me.
  6. Website URL: your GoHighLevel page-1 URL for The Argument.
  7. Build 3 more ads exactly like it: Argument - 2 (images from flexible-ad-2), Argument - 3 (flexible-ad-3), Argument - 4 (flexible-ad-4). Same 5 texts, same headline, same URL every time.

Then duplicate those 4 ads. Rename the copies Future - 1, 2, 3, 4 and change one thing only: the website URL becomes your page-1 URL for The Future.

Duplicate once more. Rename them List - 1, 2, 3, 4 and the website URL becomes your page-1 URL for The List.

That's it. 12 ads, and every funnel gets all 34 images and all 5 copies tested against it. The ad name always tells you which funnel it feeds.

This round the loading is manual because that is fastest. This week we are setting up your agent to do it for you, so next time you will not have to.
