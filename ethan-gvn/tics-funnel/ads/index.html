Ad 15 · Rapid hard blinking
G
Green Valley Nutrition
Sponsored · 🌐
···
My motor tics were never a discipline problem, but for years they looked like one. I was thirteen, standing at the bathroom counter, trying to put my toothbrush down. It did not feel right, so I picked it up and put it down again. Still wrong. Again. Then harder. Soon I was banging it against the counter with toothpaste and spit hitting the mirror. By the time my dad walked in, it looked like I was throwing a fit over a toothbrush. But inside I was asking the same question he was. Why can't you just put it down? I wanted it to stop. I could not stop it. That was the night my dad stopped treating it like a bad habit. Eleven years and $56,576 later, we finally learned what it actually was. It is not what you have been told.
