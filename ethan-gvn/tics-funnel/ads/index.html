How to launch these

Grab everything in one download: the landing pages, all 39 images sorted by angle, the copy as paste-ready text files, and step-by-step GoHighLevel instructions.

⬇ Download the full package (13 MB)

The structure: same ads, three funnels. Any of these 39 ads can drive traffic to any of the three funnels. So make three ad sets, load the same ads into each, and point each ad set at one funnel:

Ad setAds insideAll traffic goes to
1All the adsFunnel 1 (v5), page 1
2All the adsFunnel 2 (v6), page 1
3All the adsFunnel 3 (v7), page 1

That way the ads are identical everywhere, and the test tells you which funnel converts best.

Inside each ad set, we suggest flexible ads: one flexible ad per angle below (Meta takes up to 10 images per ad and finds the winners for you). Angles A and B have more than 10 images, so pick your favorites.

Every card below shows one image with its copy, so you can see exactly what goes together. "Save image" downloads that image. "Copy text" copies that angle's full primary text.

One thing to fill in: the last line of every copy ends with [your funnel link]. Paste the link of whichever funnel that ad set points at.

This round the loading is manual because that is fastest. This week we are setting up your agent to do it for you, so next time you will not have to.
