My mom spent eleven years believing she caused my motor tics.

I have asked her what she was thinking at that kitchen table at two in the morning.

It was not: what is the treatment. It was: what did I do wrong? Did I mess my kid up?

She kept the whole record. Every medication, every specialist, every dollar. Fifty-three lines across eleven years.

The answer was not in any of those lines, and it was not her fault.

It was something no doctor had even tested for.

If you are the one keeping the records in your house, this page was written for you.
