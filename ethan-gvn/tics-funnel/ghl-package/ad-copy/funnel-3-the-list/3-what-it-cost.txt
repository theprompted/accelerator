My mom did not waste a dollar of the $56,576 she spent on my motor tics. And she still got nowhere.

Not one line on her list was reckless.

An adenoidectomy, because a doctor recommended it. Genetic testing. A tonsillectomy. Immunotherapy.

Phone consults at three hundred dollars each.

Appointments she drove me hours up the coast to get to.

Every time one failed, we did not question the search. We assumed we had not searched far enough.

The problem was never her choices. Every dollar was aimed at the wrong half of the problem.

I want to tell you about the half nobody was looking at.
