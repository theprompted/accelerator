Setup Guide

Getting the Checkout Buttons Working

The funnel pages are ready. The buttons just need your Shopify products to point at. Here's exactly what to do.

What's going on

The checkout buttons on the funnel pages have placeholder links right now. That's on purpose. They need real Shopify products to point at, and those products don't exist in your store yet.

Your store currently has the Kids 350mg Oil Drops as a single bottle at $69. The funnel pages sell three tiers:

Tier Price on Page Exists in Shopify?
1 Bottle $69 Yes
3 Bottles $149 No — need to create
6 Bottles $269 No — need to create

Once you create those two bundle products, the buttons will work. The whole thing takes about 10 minutes.

Step by step

1 Create the bundle products

Go to Products in your Shopify admin.

You already have something like this set up for your gummies, the "Stock Up and Save" bundles with variant options (Single jar / 2 jars / 3 jars). Same idea here.

Create two new products for the Kids Oil Drops:

Kids 350mg Oil Drops — 3 Pack → price it at $149

Kids 350mg Oil Drops — 6 Pack → price it at $269

Tip: You can also do it as one product with three variants (1-bottle / 3-pack / 6-pack) instead of three separate products. Either way works. The gummies pattern is your template.
2 Grab the variant IDs

Once the products are live, you need the variant ID for each one. Easiest way:

Open this URL in your browser, replacing the product handle with your actual product URL slug:

https://www.greenvalleynutrition.com/products/YOUR-PRODUCT-HANDLE.json

Look for the variants section. Each variant has an id number. That's what you need.

Example: Your existing single bottle variant ID is 52672665420098. The new products will each get their own ID like this.
3 Build the checkout links

Each checkout link follows this format:

https://www.greenvalleynutrition.com/cart/VARIANT_ID:1

So you'll end up with three links, one per tier:

1 Bottle → greenvalleynutrition.com/cart/EXISTING_VARIANT_ID:1 3 Pack → greenvalleynutrition.com/cart/3PACK_VARIANT_ID:1 6 Pack → greenvalleynutrition.com/cart/6PACK_VARIANT_ID:1
4 Swap the placeholders on each funnel page

On each funnel page, find the buy buttons. There are two sets of buttons per page (one mid-page, one at the bottom), three buttons each.

Replace the placeholder text in each button's link with the real checkout URL from Step 3.

Three pages, six buttons each. Find and replace.

That's it

Once the links are swapped in, the funnel is live. Every button sends the buyer straight to a Shopify cart with the right product at the right price.

Optional

Want to test the flow right now?

If you want to click through the funnel before creating the bundles, you can temporarily point all three buttons at your existing single bottle product with different quantities:

1 Bottle → greenvalleynutrition.com/cart/52672665420098:1 3 Pack → greenvalleynutrition.com/cart/52672665420098:3 6 Pack → greenvalleynutrition.com/cart/52672665420098:6

This will work for testing the flow, but the cart will show 3 x $69.99 = $209.97 instead of $149. So it's fine for a test run, just not for sending real traffic.
