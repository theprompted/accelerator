## MANDATORY SELF-CHECK EVALUATIONS

### Ad 1 — Expression #1 (Angle: Flat Denial)

**OPENER:** "Deep breathing doesn't fix anxiety. It manages it. There's a difference."
**TARGET:** Their belief that consistent deep breathing practice will eventually cure their anxiety
**READER REACTION:** "Wait — I've been doing deep breathing for years thinking it would eventually stick. Are you saying it's just... maintenance forever?"
**GOLD STANDARD:** PASS — Reader feels their years of effort and hope are being challenged. This is identity-threatening because they've invested in the belief that "practice = progress."
**FUCK YOU TEST:** PASS — Reader's instinct is to argue: "But my therapist said..." "But the apps all say..." "But I've felt better when I do it..."
**TURN TEST:** PASS — "Your breathing exercises work exactly as designed. The problem is what they were designed to do — override a system that needs fuel, not force."
**FAIL PATTERN:** N/A
**VERDICT:** PASS

---

### Ad 2 — Expression #5 (Angle: Flip)

**OPENER:** "The more you practice calming techniques, the more dependent you become on them. That's not healing. That's a maintenance contract."
**TARGET:** Their belief that diligent practice leads to less need for practice — that they're building internal capacity
**READER REACTION:** "What? No — I'm getting better at handling things. Each session is building something... right?" Defensiveness mixed with a flicker of recognition.
**GOLD STANDARD:** PASS — Attacks their identity as someone working toward independence from anxiety. Implies their effort is creating the opposite of what they want.
**FUCK YOU TEST:** PASS — Immediate defensive reaction. They want to argue that their routine is helping, not creating dependency.
**TURN TEST:** PASS — "Healing means your body handles anxiety automatically. Maintenance means you have to keep doing something. One is recovering. The other is coping."
**FAIL PATTERN:** N/A
**VERDICT:** PASS

---

### Ad 3 — Expression #9 (Angle: Attack Advice)

**OPENER:** "Everyone told you to regulate your nervous system. Nobody told you it was running on empty."
**TARGET:** Their trust in the advice they've followed — the "regulate your nervous system" mantra from therapists, apps, influencers
**READER REACTION:** "But that's what everyone says to do... Is the advice I've been following actually incomplete? Did I miss something?"
**GOLD STANDARD:** PASS — Attacks their informed-consumer identity. They did the research, followed the experts, and this says they were missing a crucial piece.
**FUCK YOU TEST:** PASS — Defensiveness about their past research and adherence to expert advice. "But I WAS doing what they said!"
**TURN TEST:** PASS — "The advice wasn't wrong. It was incomplete. They gave you the technique without telling you what it runs on."
**FAIL PATTERN:** N/A
**VERDICT:** PASS

---

### Ad 4 — Expression #13 (Angle: Attack Trade-Off)

**OPENER:** "You traded 10 minutes every morning for a few hours of relief. That's not a solution. That's a shift rate."
**TARGET:** Their belief that their morning routine is a reasonable investment in their wellbeing
**READER REACTION:** "A shift rate? I thought I was taking care of myself. Is this... is this just like punching a clock for my own mental health?"
**GOLD STANDARD:** PASS — Reframes their self-care ritual as something transactional and endless, attacking their identity as someone with a "solution."
**FUCK YOU TEST:** PASS — Defensiveness: "My morning routine IS self-care. This is me investing in myself."
**TURN TEST:** PASS — "A solution means the problem gets smaller over time. A maintenance contract means you pay the same amount forever."
**FAIL PATTERN:** N/A
**VERDICT:** PASS

---

### Ad 5 — Expression #17 (Angle: Quit Proof)

**OPENER:** "You've been meditating for years. If it was going to work, it would have worked by now."
**TARGET:** Their hope that continued practice will eventually lead to breakthrough
**READER REACTION:** "But... maybe I just need more time? More consistency?" A painful recognition that surfaces the doubt they've been suppressing.
**GOLD STANDARD:** PASS — Directly attacks the identity of "someone who is on the path" — suggests the path itself is wrong, not just the walking.
**FUCK YOU TEST:** PASS — Strong defensive reaction. They want to explain why it hasn't worked YET — life stress, inconsistency, not the right app.
**TURN TEST:** PASS — "The techniques were never designed to fix the underlying problem. They were designed to help you cope with a system that was running out of fuel."
**FAIL PATTERN:** N/A
**VERDICT:** PASS

---

### Ad 6 — Expression #21 (Angle: Real Variable)

**OPENER:** "It was never about how you breathe. It was about what's missing when you're not breathing."
**TARGET:** Their belief that technique is the variable — that better/more consistent breathing is the answer
**READER REACTION:** "Missing? What's missing? I've been focusing on HOW to breathe and you're saying there's something else entirely?"
**GOLD STANDARD:** PASS — Attacks their entire mental model of the problem. They've been optimizing the wrong variable.
**FUCK YOU TEST:** PASS — Defensive: "But all the research says breathing IS the thing. I've read the studies."
**TURN TEST:** PASS — "Breathing techniques activate a system. If that system is depleted, no amount of technique can substitute for what's gone."
**FAIL PATTERN:** N/A
**VERDICT:** PASS

---

### Ad 7 — Expression #25 (Angle: Guilt)

**OPENER:** "You weren't failing at calming techniques. You were using the right tool for the wrong problem."
**TARGET:** Their guilt about not being consistent enough, disciplined enough, committed enough
**READER REACTION:** "Right tool, wrong problem? So... it wasn't my fault it didn't work?" This is identity-protecting BUT the "wrong problem" part is disruptive.
**GOLD STANDARD:** PASS — Creates tension by validating effort while invalidating the entire framework. Reader feels relief mixed with "wait, what problem SHOULD I be solving?"
**FUCK YOU TEST:** PASS (inverted) — Reader doesn't think "fuck you" but feels a destabilizing mix of relief and confusion. The guilt lift creates enough cognitive dissonance to need resolution.
**TURN TEST:** PASS — "The tool wasn't broken. Your technique wasn't broken. The problem you were solving for wasn't the real problem."
**FAIL PATTERN:** N/A — Not a compliment in disguise because it still attacks their understanding of the problem
**VERDICT:** PASS

---

### Ad 8 — Expression #29 (Angle: Protect)

**OPENER:** "Your body has a calming system that was supposed to handle all of this automatically. It's been running on empty, doing the best it can with nothing left."
**TARGET:** Their belief that they're broken, weak, or fundamentally anxious as a personality trait
**READER REACTION:** "Wait — there's a system that's supposed to do this for me? I'm not just... wired anxious?" A mix of relief and "why didn't anyone tell me this?"
**GOLD STANDARD:** PASS — Attacks their resigned identity as "an anxious person" by introducing a mechanism they didn't know existed
**FUCK YOU TEST:** PASS (inverted) — This is more "holy shit, really?" than "fuck you." Still triggers the need to resolve.
**TURN TEST:** PASS — "The system is real. The depletion is real. And the fact that it can be refilled is something almost no one talks about."
**FAIL PATTERN:** N/A
**VERDICT:** PASS

### Ad 9 — Expression #33 (Angle: Identity)

**OPENER:** "You knew deep down the techniques weren't fixing anything. You just couldn't prove it."
**TARGET:** The cognitive dissonance they've been carrying — suspecting the techniques don't really work while continuing to do them
**READER REACTION:** "...yeah. I did know. I just felt crazy saying it because everyone says they work." Validation of a suppressed doubt.
**GOLD STANDARD:** PASS — Attacks by validating their secret doubt, which forces them to confront why they continued anyway
**FUCK YOU TEST:** PASS — More "holy shit, how did you know" but with underlying defensiveness about why they kept going
**TURN TEST:** PASS — "The techniques work for what they're designed to do. They just weren't designed to fix the underlying problem. Your instinct was right."
**FAIL PATTERN:** N/A
**VERDICT:** PASS

### Ad 10 — Expression #37 (Angle: Science)

**OPENER:** "A 2023 Stanford study found the calming effect of deep breathing only holds if you practice every single day. Miss a day, lose the momentum. That's maintenance, not repair."
**TARGET:** Their belief that they're building something permanent through practice
**READER REACTION:** "Every single day? But I thought... I was getting BETTER, not just maintaining." The data contradicts their hope.
**GOLD STANDARD:** PASS — Uses credible research to attack their investment in the "cumulative progress" narrative
**FUCK YOU TEST:** PASS — Defensive: "But I HAVE seen improvement. Maybe my experience is different from the study."
**TURN TEST:** PASS — "The study isn't saying breathing doesn't work. It's saying the effect is dose-dependent. You have to keep dosing forever. That's not recovery."
**FAIL PATTERN:** N/A
**VERDICT:** PASS
