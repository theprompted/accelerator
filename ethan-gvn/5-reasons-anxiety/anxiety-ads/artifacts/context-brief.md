## KEY CLAIMS / EVIDENCE SECTIONS

### R1: Deep Breathing Is a Manual Override — Not a Fix
- 2023 Stanford study: Cyclic sighing reduced anxiety more than mindfulness meditation
- BUT: Effect required daily practice to hold — miss a day, lose the momentum
- Meta-analysis across 12 trials: Modest effect, only while practicing
- Metaphor: Hand-pumping brake line in a car with no fluid

### R2: Your Nervous System Isn't Dysregulated — It's Depleted
- 2016 Dr. Ethan Russo landmark paper on endocannabinoid deficiency
- Chronic stress increases FAAH enzyme → breaks down anandamide faster
- 2014 study: Central anandamide levels predict anxiety — lower = higher anxiety
- Metaphor: Brake fluid is gone — pressing pedal harder doesn't fix empty reservoir

### R3: Meditation Apps Train You to Be a Calmer Passenger — Not a Better Driver
- 2022 meta-analysis: Calm/Headspace produced "modest but consistent" reductions (effect sizes 0.23-0.32)
- Gone when you stop using them
- Meditation assumes calming system works, just needs better inputs — wrong assumption
- Metaphor: Observing the car rolling downhill more peacefully doesn't stop the car

### R4: Your Body Has an Automatic Calming System That Most Influencers Never Mention
- Endocannabinoid system = "shock absorber for emotions"
- Anandamide = primary molecule
- FAAH C385A genetic variant: People with slower anandamide breakdown show reduced anxiety
- Rimonabant (weight-loss drug) blocked this system → depression/anxiety spiked → pulled from market
- Block the system = anxiety spikes (proof from the negative)

### R5: The Longer You Manually Override, the More Dependent You Become
- Chronic depletion gets worse over time — brain loses receptors, pathways narrow
- Manual techniques are effort-dependent (dose-based) vs. automatic regulation (always-on)
- Creates psychological dependency: "I am someone who needs to DO something to be calm"
- That belief is wrong — you're not weak, your system ran out of fuel
