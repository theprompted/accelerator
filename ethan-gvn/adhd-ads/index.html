Violation Openers

The opening line of each ad is designed to sound wrong. That's not a mistake — it's the mechanism.

By line 2-3, the nuance makes the opener true in a way the reader hadn't considered. Now they have tension they can't resolve without clicking.

Every ad attacks the system — the advice, the framework, the conversation — never the parent. The reader should finish feeling like their instincts were right all along. The product is never named. The brand is never mentioned. The ad's only job is the click. The funnel does the rest.
