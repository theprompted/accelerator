## MANDATORY SELF-CHECKS (Full Evaluations)

### Expression 1 — #2: Flat Denial

**OPENER:** "Natural fibers aren't better. They're just more expensive."
**TARGET:** The belief that "natural = better" — the identity as someone who chose natural for quality reasons
**READER REACTION:** "Wait, what? I specifically chose natural because it IS better. Are you saying I wasted money?"

**GOLD STANDARD:** PASS
Attacks their choice, their research, their purchasing identity. They feel defensive about the premium they paid.

**FUCK YOU TEST:** PASS
Reader thinks "fuck you, natural IS better — that's why I paid extra." They want to argue back.

**TURN TEST:** PASS
Turn: "Alpaca evolved to keep animals alive in freezing mountains. Standing still. Not to survive your commute and your washing machine."
Natural turn — you're not attacking natural fibers, you're showing they were designed for a different purpose.

**FAIL PATTERN:** N/A
**VERDICT:** ✓ PASS (3/3 tests)

### Expression 2 — #7: Flip

**OPENER:** "That 'soft' wool sock is making your feet sweat more, not less."
**TARGET:** The belief that softness = comfort. Their identity as someone who chose premium for comfort.
**READER REACTION:** "What? I chose soft specifically for comfort. My feet feel better in soft socks."

**GOLD STANDARD:** PASS
Directly contradicts the value proposition they paid for. They feel their judgment is questioned.

**FUCK YOU TEST:** PASS
"Fuck you, my soft socks ARE more comfortable." Immediate defensiveness.

**TURN TEST:** PASS
Turn: "Your feet produce half a pint of sweat per day. Wool absorbs it. Then holds onto it. That's why the sock that felt amazing at 9am feels different by 5pm."
Turn shows softness and comfort aren't the same thing — their instinct wasn't wrong, they just had incomplete information.

**FAIL PATTERN:** N/A
**VERDICT:** ✓ PASS (3/3 tests)
