# Mat & Vic's Funnel — Master Expression Bank

**Funnel:** "5 Reasons You Shouldn't Buy Premium Wool or Alpaca Socks"
**One Belief Attacked:** "Natural fibers (alpaca, merino, cashmere) are better than engineered blends — if I want quality socks, I should pay premium for natural."
**Identity Defended:** The smart consumer who did their research, values quality, and won't be fooled by marketing stories. They weren't wrong to care about quality — they were handed the wrong definition of it.
**Identity Attacked:** The premium sock industry's "natural equals better" narrative and the assumption that high price signals high performance.
**Resolution Withheld:** The 78/20 Formula (Mat & Vic's cotton-polyamide blend) — never named in ads.

## BATCH PLANNING RULES

1. Default batch size is **20 ads**. Each batch pulls from all 10 angles — 2 per angle. For 10 ads: 1 per angle. Every angle must be represented at least once.

2. Each expression has a pre-assigned evidence pairing (R1-R5). Within a batch of 10, aim for at least 4 different evidence pairings.

3. **Evidence key:**
   - R1: "Natural" doesn't mean better — designed for animals not humans (Appeal to Nature Fallacy)
   - R2: Softest sock at 9am is sweatiest by 5pm (moisture management, softness ≠ comfort)
   - R3: "Special care required" is design failure (care instructions, washing machine test)
   - R4: Premium fibers wear out faster (pilling, felting, thinning, durability perception)
   - R5: "100% pure" is a compromise (blends outperform, 78/20 Formula)

4. Images stay in the **sock world**: sock drawer, worn socks, laundry, care labels, feet in socks, sock shopping. **All images must be sock-visible for Meta algorithm targeting.** Heavy on contradiction visuals.

5. Each batch must use unique image concepts — check batch-history.md to avoid repeating visual setups.

6. After each batch, append used expression numbers, evidence pairings, AND image concepts to batch-history.md.

7. No expression number is ever reused across batches.

8. **MANDATORY IMAGE:** 1 of every 10 ads must use this concept: "Polished woman (35, blazer/nice dress) looking down at her feet — dingy, pilled, hole-ridden socks that don't match her put-together appearance. Mild embarrassment."
