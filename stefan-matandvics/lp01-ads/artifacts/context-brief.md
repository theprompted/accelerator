## CORE THESIS

You choose socks by how they feel in 3 seconds (the store touch test). But comfort is what happens over 8 hours. The softness you feel at the shelf becomes sweat by hour 3 and holes by week 3. "Weich" (soft) and "bequem" (comfortable) are not the same thing — and the sock industry exploits this confusion.

## KEY CLAIMS / EVIDENCE SECTIONS

### R1: The 3-Second Trick — How Stores Exploit Your Touch Instinct
- "Du fällst auf den Weichheits-Trick herein. Jedes. Einzelne. Mal."
- 3 seconds of touch = purchase decision
- Softness triggers "this must be comfortable" assumption
- Industry knows this and optimizes for shelf-feel, not wear-feel

### R2: Soft at 9am, Sweaty by 5pm — The Moisture Problem
- "Die weichste Socke um 9 Uhr ist die schweißigste um 17 Uhr"
- Soft fibers absorb moisture and hold it
- Feet produce ~250ml sweat per day
- "Wie eine Daunendecke im Juli" — cozy at first, unbearable by midday
- "Deine Füße schmoren stundenlang im eigenen Saft"

### R3: The 3-Week Reality Check — Durability Failure
- After 3 weeks: holes at heels, shape lost
- Soft fibers = structurally weak fibers
- "Schon wieder neue kaufen" — the replacement cycle
- Softness and durability are inversely correlated

### R4: First-Touch vs. All-Day — The Category Error
- "Du hast nicht nach Komfort ausgewählt. Du hast nach First-Touch-Weichheit ausgewählt."
- "Das ist NICHT dasselbe."
- Comfort is a system: moisture management, temperature regulation, 8-hour performance
- First-touch is a single variable optimized for the store, not your life

### R5: The Industry Knows — Deliberate Exploitation
- "Die Sockenindustrie weiß das — und nutzt es aus."
- They optimize for shelf appeal, not daily wear
- "Weich = bequem" is a manufactured confusion
- Consumer psychology weaponized against you
