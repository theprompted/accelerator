Unit Economics & Pricing

Verdict: Good starting point. Ship it.

Pricing doesn't need to be locked in forever. Go with what you've decided now and experiment over time. The numbers will tell you what's working.

Getting to 100 ads/week

Not everything has to be net-new — a portion can be iterative.

For example, if you're doing 100 ads, it can be made up of 10 unique concepts + 10 iterations of each:

  • Different sock colors
  • Different backgrounds
  • Different hand (male/female)
  • Different hole location
  • Different lighting conditions

Same concept, multiple executions. This is how you scale without burning out on net-new ideas.
