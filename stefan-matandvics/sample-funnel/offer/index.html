Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between Mat & Vic's and regular cotton socks?

Most cotton socks use 60-70% cotton padded with cheap polyester.

Mat & Vic's uses 78% cotton - nearly 80% - combined with Performance Polyamide instead of polyester.

That means more natural softness, better moisture management, and durability that actually lasts.

It's not just cotton socks. It's The 78/20 Formula.

Can I really wash them any way I want?

Yes.

Hot water, cold water, whatever temperature you use for everything else.

Tumble dry, air dry, your choice.

Throw them in with your jeans, towels, and t-shirts.

That's what All-Wash Construction means - socks that survive your life without special treatment.

How do they compare to alpaca or merino wool socks?

Premium fiber socks require special care - gentle cycles, air drying, reshaping while damp.

Miss one step and they shrink, felt, or lose their shape.

Mat & Vic's was engineered for real life.

You get comparable comfort with zero maintenance drama.

Plus, you can buy 10+ pairs for the price of one alpaca sock.

Why is The 78/20 Formula better than 100% cotton?

100% cotton socks wear out fast.

They stretch, lose shape, and develop holes at the heel.

The 20% polyamide in Mat & Vic's provides the durability and shape retention that cotton alone can't deliver.

It's the combination that makes the difference.

How long do Mat & Vic's socks last?

With The 78/20 Formula and normal use, most customers report 1-2+ years of regular wear before any noticeable degradation.

That's significantly longer than commodity socks and comparable to (or better than) premium fiber socks - without the special care requirements.
