Principles to Keep in Mind

Avoid Overcorrecting

Today's spend (or lack of spend) impacts sales the next 10-14 days. A tight day like Feb 28 (3.3% CM) could be setting you up for stronger days ahead. Don't go hard on overcorrections.

Budget Is the First Lever

Since you're spending past your campaign budgets, it means volume is being left on the table. That's the first variable to manipulate — raise budgets to unlock more profitable spend.

Tighten Underperformers, Don't Kill Them

To get closer to your 20% rolling 7-day average CM target, look at ad sets below target and tighten by about 15%. Let them keep running. They may be helping fill the funnel for other ads.

Be a Surgeon

Large overcorrections can make the next few days look really good, then cause things to die out. Small, precise adjustments. Don't swing the axe.
