Ad Loading Cadence

Your skill of making ads is good enough now that ads are getting spend instead of just 0 in spend, and you're at a stage where you get ads that spend a decent amount on one day but little the next.

This can make your ad spend unprofitable if you load frequently because the bursts add up.

Loading in bigger batches, less frequently, can contain the spikes where you get a burst one day on batches of ads that don't get spend the next.

You can batch load once or twice a week. If once a week, do Mondays. If twice a week, do Mon & Thurs.

I typically do once a week now. It's your call.

Checkout Permalinks

Instead of going to PDP on the advertorial funnels and ecom LPs, use checkout permalinks.

This can help with conversion rate. Basically each tier of the offer goes straight to checkout as opposed to requiring the consumer to go the PDP, add to cart, then checkout.

Focus & Congruence

Focus and congruence is your next stage.

Right now you have a good amount of variety in your ads despite going to the same landing page (the advertorial mostly, some to the ecom LP).

What I would do is go through the ad account and look at spend last 7 days, sort by amount spent, and look at the ads that got $20 in spend or more.

And get a sense of whether most of the spend is going to intimacy issues, stretching, male vs. female, etc. - because these are all different angles themselves.

Once you get a good sense of which one is getting most spend, then you can make everything congruent and focused on that problem. From the ads, to the advertorial, to the ecom LPs that are built to support retargeting (via broad targeting).
