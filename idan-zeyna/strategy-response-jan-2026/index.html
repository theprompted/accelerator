Ad Loading Cadence

Your skill of making ads is good enough now that ads are getting spend instead of just 0 in spend.

You're at a stage where you get ads that spend a decent amount on one day but little the next. This can make your ad spend unprofitable if you load frequently because the bursts add up.

Loading in bigger batches, less frequently, can contain the spikes where you get a burst one day on batches of ads that don't get spend the next.
Recommendation

Batch load once or twice a week.

  • 1 If once a week: Mondays
  • 2 If twice a week: Mon & Thurs

I typically do once a week now. It's your call.

Skip the PDP — Go Straight to Checkout

Instead of going to PDP on the advertorial funnels and ecom LPs, use checkout permalinks.

This can help with conversion rate. Each tier of the offer goes straight to checkout instead of requiring the consumer to go to PDP → add to cart → checkout.

Shopify Documentation

help.shopify.com/en/manual/checkout-settings/cart-permalink

https://yourstore.com/cart/VARIANT_ID:QUANTITY?checkout=true

You'll need the variant IDs for each product/tier from your Shopify admin.

Focus & Congruence

This is your next stage.

Right now you have a good amount of variety in your ads despite going to the same landing page (the advertorial mostly, some to the ecom LP).

Action Steps
  • 1 Go through the ad account and look at spend last 7 days, sort by amount spent
  • 2 Look at the ads that got $20+ in spend
  • 3 Get a sense of whether most spend is going to: intimacy issues, stretching, male vs. female, etc.
  • 4 Once you identify which angle is getting most spend, make everything congruent and focused on that problem

From the ads, to the advertorial, to the ecom LPs that are built to support retargeting (via broad targeting).

Find the winning angle, then make everything point at it.

Quick Answers

"Should I try raising budget on ad sets that sold to see where it gets spend? Does going over the calculated CPA make sense to check?"

Yes, absolutely you can raise because you have lots of room. Just keep the budget contained so it doesn't spend through like crazy—you want to land on a number that gets a good amount of spend through.

"The AI accidentally created a missing hand in the image. Could this be a pattern interrupt?"

Knou did a batch of these—they're quite interesting. That's one way to catch attention, and you can continue this route as a format.

Another approach is subtle pattern interrupts. For example, for the intimacy angle: husband and wife sleeping head-to-toe instead of head on the same side. It's not a wild deviation, but enough that it's a pattern interrupt.
