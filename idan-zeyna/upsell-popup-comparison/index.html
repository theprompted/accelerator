What it looks like
The problem
  • ⚠️ Generic discount frame. "Special offer — get a second ViroCore at 50% off." Practical. Doesn't pull. The man buying a PE device isn't thinking about a backup for travel.
  • ⚠️ Neutral decline link. "No thanks, continue with one unit." Zero identity cost. Men scroll past it without feeling anything.
  • ⚠️ Copy speaks to logistics, not identity. The man buying ViroCore is thinking about who he's becoming. The popup speaks to his calendar, not his character.
v2 Phase 1 — Copy rewrite only Same offer, new frame
What changed
  • ✦ New headline — transformation arc. "Here's How You Can Go From Lasting 60 Seconds To 60 Minutes." From X to Y, specific and concrete. Speaks directly to why he bought in the first place.
  • ✦ Body copy built around an archetype. The man who doesn't leave anything to chance. He controls his environment the same way he controls himself. The second ViroCore isn't a backup — it's a statement.
  • ✦ "She will know the difference." Short. Final. Her lens. She's in the back of his mind for everything, even when the copy is speaking directly to him.
  • ✦ Identity-cost decline link. "No thanks, 60 seconds is fine for now." Most decline links are invisible. This one lands.
  • — Nothing changed: The offer (₪200 / 50% off), price box, layout. Phase 1 is always copy. Test this first before adding complexity.
