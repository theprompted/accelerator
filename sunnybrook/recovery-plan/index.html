After Surgery — What Comes Next

Healing happens in stages. How long each stage takes depends on how the body recovers. There is no fixed timeline — the medical team decides when it's time for the next step.

Options for After Rehab

A discharge planner at the hospital or rehab centre helps figure out the right plan. That's their job. They look at how the patient is doing and what kind of support is needed.

Home with visiting helpers

Government-funded home care can provide free visits — help with bathing, meals, medicines, and exercises. Private caregivers can also be hired for extra hours if needed.

Short stay at a retirement home

Some places offer stays of a few weeks — private room, meals, and staff nearby around the clock. This costs money.

Recovery bed in a care home

Some long-term care homes have beds for short-term surgery recovery. Free, with 24-hour care and daily physiotherapy. May have a waiting list.

Stay with family

Possible for a short time. A caregiver would still be arranged to help during the day.

Organizations Andrew Is Looking Into

Free / Government-Funded
FREE — OHIP
Ontario Health atHome
The provincial home care program. Provides PSW visits (bathing, dressing, meals), nursing visits (wound care, medications), and physiotherapy at home. Usually covers a couple of visits per day, not all-day coverage. The hospital or rehab centre arranges this before discharge.
Phone: 310-2222 (no area code needed)
FREE — OHIP
Convalescent Care Beds (York Region)
Short-stay recovery beds inside long-term care homes. 24-hour nursing, physiotherapy twice daily, meals included. Managed by Ontario Health atHome. Closest locations: AgeCare Aurora (32 Mill St), Maple Health Centre (10424 Keele St), Newmarket Health Centre (194 Eagle St). May have a waiting list.
Phone: 905-895-1240 or 416-222-2241
Private Home Care (~$28-35/hr)

These send caregivers to the home for as many hours as needed. They help with bathing, meals, walking, medication reminders, and companionship. If a caregiver calls in sick, the agency sends a replacement.

Canada's largest home care company. 13,000+ staff across 100+ locations. Covers Markham, Richmond Hill, Thornhill, Vaughan, Stouffville, Aurora, and Newmarket. Also delivers government-funded home care, so they know the system well.

Non-profit home care organization with an office in Markham (25 Centurian Dr, Unit 104). Provides nursing, PSW, physiotherapy, occupational therapy, and dietetics. Also runs Elizz, a caregiver support service that helps families navigate recovery planning.

Premium home care provider, 20 years in business. Specifically trains caregivers for cardiac recovery — heart-healthy diet, monitoring for complications, mobility support after heart surgery. Serves Toronto, Markham, and North York. 4.8-star rating.
